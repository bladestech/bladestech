## 🌡️ EZvac and its ecosystem

My main project: **[EZvac](https://github.com/EZvac-Plugin/EZvac)**, real
heating, cooling and indoor climate for Paper and Purpur servers. It started
as a few thermostat signs and grew into thermostats, heat pumps and vents,
a living climate with seasons and wind, air quality, refrigeration, weather
station forecasts, screens and a HUD, and a guided tutorial, all in the
[EZvac-Plugin](https://github.com/EZvac-Plugin) organisation.

| Project | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| [EZvac](https://github.com/EZvac-Plugin/EZvac) | Real heating, cooling and indoor climate for Minecraft servers |
| [EZclimates](https://github.com/EZvac-Plugin/EZclimates) | Seasons you can see and feel: snow, turning leaves, planned weather, body temperature, food that spoils |
| [EZdebug+](https://github.com/EZvac-Plugin/EZdebugPlus) | See every EZvac system at work, through walls |
| [MapPeek](https://github.com/EZvac-Plugin/MapPeek) | EZvac's weather, wind and air quality on squaremap and BlueMap |
